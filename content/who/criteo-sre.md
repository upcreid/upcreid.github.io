I joined the Criteo Webapps scope as an SRE. Keeping a large-scale platform reliable is a daily job, and most of it happens before anything breaks. Between two incidents, I write code, migrate systems and try to make deployments boring.

## What I did

- Keep the Criteo Client API available and healthy
- Write and maintain services in C#, Go and Python
- Take part in the Flink migration
- Build continuous deployment so changes ship safely
